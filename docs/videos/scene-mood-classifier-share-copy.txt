Scene Mood Classifier: a CLIP plus Wav2Vec2 fusion model that scores the mood of a video or image-and-audio pair, shipped as one container image to GCP, AWS and Azure with Prometheus and Grafana monitoring, Discord alerting and GitHub Actions CI/CD. Recovery paths were tested against forced crashes, full wipes and network drops, with recovery time treated as a performance metric. Live as two Hugging Face Spaces.
