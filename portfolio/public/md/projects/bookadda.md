# BookADDA: peer-to-peer book rental platform

Full-stack web app for students.

> An online platform where students buy, rent or sell books with fellow students, with a search-first interface and a Flask backend.

- Kind: Web
- Period: Jun 2020 to Nov 2020 (completed)
- Affiliation: PES Modern College of Engineering
- Team: Shreya Boyane with three teammates

## Problem

Textbooks are expensive and campus book exchange was informal and slow.

## Approach

Designed and implemented the entire frontend in HTML, CSS, Bootstrap and JavaScript and integrated it with a Python Flask and MySQL backend.

## Results

- Launched a working platform that received positive feedback for ease of use and search.

## Key numbers

- Team size: 4

## Stack

HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, JavaScript, Flask, MySQL
